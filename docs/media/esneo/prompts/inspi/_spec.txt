Website design concept rendered as one flat, tall, full-page desktop screenshot (no browser chrome, no device, no perspective), at the level of an Awwwards Site of the Day. Brand: "esneo", a lowercase script wordmark; a French AI SaaS for IT consulting firms (ESN) that turns consultant CVs and client mission briefs into a weighted positioning score, where a human validates every AI proposal ("L'IA propose, vous validez."). All visible copy in French. Strict palette: near-black graphite #0d0e11, pure white, light grey #f1f3f6, a single azure blue #3466e6 accent; small green, amber and red status colours appear only inside interface fragments. One grotesk typeface in the spirit of Hanken Grotesk or Neue Haas Grotesk, medium weight, very tight tracking, display sizes huge and confident, body text small and calm, left-aligned, sentence case. Absolutely no gradients, no glow, no glassmorphism, no rows of icon cards, no pill badges above headlines, no monospace, no 01/02/03 numbering, no hairline grid lines. Real product interface fragments at true size, crisp and readable: a positioning card "Camille Durand · Tech lead Java · Assurance, La Défense · 84/100 · Confiance élevée" listing points "Point fort", "Écart", "À clarifier" each with small "Valider" and "Écarter" buttons and a blue "Générer le CV adapté" button; a status board of consultants with status blocks "Validé", "À valider", "En cours 3/5", "Échec". Editorial documentary photography of Paris business life with a cool graphite and azure grade: La Défense towers at blue hour, a Monday staffing meeting, a consultant crossing a bank lobby, hands reviewing a printed skills dossier.
