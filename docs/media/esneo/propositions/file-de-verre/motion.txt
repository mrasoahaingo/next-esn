Website design concept for "esneo", rendered as a flat, crisp screenshot of a real, finished website (no browser chrome, no device frame, no perspective, no desk scene around it unless asked), at the level of an Awwwards Site of the Day and of Apple's own product pages. esneo is a French AI tool for headhunters, IT account managers and recruiters (ESN and recruitment firms): it extracts CVs, breaks down client mission briefs, proposes a scored positioning of a candidate against a mission, and prepares the tailored CV and e-mails; a human validates every AI proposal. Variant "La file de verre": a visionOS-like spatial scene. A dark, calm room at night with a wide window onto an out-of-focus city (cool lights, no logos, no signage); in this space floats a single row of tall rounded glass windows receding in depth, like a stack of cards seen at an angle. The window in front is in focus, clear and lit from within by azure light, its specular rim bright; the windows behind are smaller, more frosted, dimmer and slightly blurred by depth of field. The light of the front window spills as a soft azure reflection onto a dark polished floor. Each window is one step of the work: the CV read, the mission broken down, the positioning, the tailored CV and e-mails; at the far end, two windows still fully frosted carry the word "Bientôt": the prospecting tool and connecting your own Claude assistant.

Dark first. Palette: night #07080b and graphite #0d0e11, deep blue-black #0b1020 for the room, glass tinted rgba(255,255,255,0.06 to 0.14), azure #3466e6 as the action colour and the light inside the front window, lighter azure glow #6f94ff only where light spills, white #f5f7fa text, soft grey #a6abb4 secondary text. Status colours only inside interface rows: green #2fa36b "Validé", amber #e9b54c "À valider", red #e0533c "Échec".

Typography: Geist for everything, the product's own typeface: large headlines in medium weight with tight negative tracking, sentence case, never more than two lines; body and interface small and calm, slightly heavier and slightly tracked on glass for legibility. Buttons: a solid azure capsule for the main action, a clear glass capsule for the secondary one.

Rules: sophistication through restraint, typography and the quality of the light. The glow always comes from a visible source (the front glass window, the city behind the window), never a floating halo. Absolutely no purple, no violet-to-blue or rainbow gradients, no neon, no glowing orbs, no network or node illustrations, no floating statistic chips, no bento grid, no rows of icon cards, no pill-shaped labels above headlines, no monospace, no emoji, no stock 3D blobs. No customer logos, no testimonials, no star ratings, no usage statistics, no prices. Features not yet available are always marked with the plain word "Bientôt". People and names are fictional examples.
