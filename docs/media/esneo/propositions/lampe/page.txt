Website design concept for "esneo", rendered as a flat, crisp screenshot of a real, finished website (no browser chrome, no device frame, no perspective, no desk scene around it unless asked), at the level of an Awwwards Site of the Day and of Apple's own product pages. esneo is a French AI tool for headhunters, IT account managers and recruiters (ESN and recruitment firms): it extracts CVs, breaks down client mission briefs, proposes a scored positioning of a candidate against a mission, and prepares the tailored CV and e-mails; a human validates every AI proposal. Its promise: less paperwork, more time for real human contact with candidates, consultants and clients. All visible copy is in French, spelled exactly as quoted, with French accents. The brand appears only as the lowercase wordmark "esneo" in a heavy brush-script style, in white; no other brand, logo or company name anywhere; the word "Claude", when asked, is plain text only, never a logo or symbol.

Variant "La lampe": a dark still life at night. A walnut desk in an otherwise dark office, lit by one warm brass desk lamp; on the desk a loose pile of printed CVs and client mission briefs (no readable text); behind, a window with faint cool city lights out of focus. Above the paper, the AI's work floats as iOS 26 "Liquid Glass" panels: thick translucent glass slabs with real refraction, a background blur, thin specular rims that catch the warm lamp on one edge and the cool azure light on the other, soft contact shadows on the desk. Each glass panel gives off a faint azure light of its own that falls on the paper below, as if the paper were turning into glass. Cinematic, photographic, quiet: a product shot of light and material.

Dark first. Palette: night #07080b and graphite #0d0e11 for the darkness, warm lamp light #f2a65a (the one warm source), azure #3466e6 as the action colour and the cool light of the glass, lighter azure glow #6f94ff only where the glass casts light, white #f5f7fa text, soft grey #a6abb4 secondary text. Status colours only inside interface rows: green #2fa36b "Validé", amber #e9b54c "À valider", red #e0533c "Échec".

Typography: headlines in an elegant high-contrast serif in the spirit of Instrument Serif (regular weight, large, tight, sentence case, never more than two lines); body and interface in Inter, slightly heavier and slightly tracked on glass for legibility. Buttons: a solid azure capsule for the main action, a clear glass capsule for the secondary one.

Rules: sophistication through restraint, typography and the quality of the light. The glow always comes from a visible source (the lamp, the glass panels, the window), never a floating halo. Absolutely no purple, no violet-to-blue or rainbow gradients, no neon, no glowing orbs, no network or node illustrations, no floating statistic chips, no bento grid, no rows of icon cards, no pill-shaped labels above headlines, no monospace, no emoji, no stock 3D blobs. No customer logos, no testimonials, no star ratings, no usage statistics, no prices. Features not yet available are always marked with the plain word "Bientôt". People and names are fictional examples.
