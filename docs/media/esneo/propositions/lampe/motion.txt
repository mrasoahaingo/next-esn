Buttons: a solid azure capsule for the main action, a clear glass capsule for the secondary one.

Rules: sophistication through restraint, typography and the quality of the light. The glow always comes from a visible source (the lamp, the glass panels, the window), never a floating halo. No customer logos, no testimonials, no star ratings, no usage statistics, no prices. Features not yet available are always marked with the plain word "Bientôt".
