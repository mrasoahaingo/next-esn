Website design concept for "esneo", rendered as a flat, crisp screenshot of a real, finished website (no browser chrome, no device frame, no perspective, no desk scene around it unless asked), at the level of an Awwwards Site of the Day and of Apple's own product pages. esneo is a French AI tool for headhunters, IT account managers and recruiters (ESN and recruitment firms): it extracts CVs, breaks down client mission briefs, proposes a scored positioning of a candidate against a mission, and prepares the tailored CV and e-mails; a human validates every AI proposal. Its promise: less paperwork, more time for real human contact with candidates, consultants and clients. All visible copy is in French, spelled exactly as quoted, with French accents. The brand appears only as the lowercase wordmark "esneo" in a heavy brush-script style, in white; no other brand, logo or company name anywhere; the word "Claude", when asked, is plain text only, never a logo or symbol.

Variant "Le flou se lève": the paperwork is a frosted glass pane laid over a human moment. Behind everything is a real documentary photograph: a recruiter and a candidate talking face to face at a small table in a calm hotel lobby or café at dusk, warm pendant lamps glowing amber, a tall window onto a blue-hour city (no logos, no signage, no readable text). In front of it, iOS 26 "Liquid Glass" panels: translucent dark glass with a strong background blur and saturation, a thin bright specular rim along the top-left edge where the lamps catch it, soft inner highlights, gentle depth shadows, real refraction of the lights behind. The heavier the paperwork, the stronger the frost: where the AI has finished, the glass clears and the people behind come into focus.

Dark first. Palette: night #07080b and graphite #0d0e11, glass tinted rgba(13,14,17,0.45) to rgba(255,255,255,0.08), azure #3466e6 as the one action colour and the cool tint of the light, a lighter azure glow #6f94ff only where light spills from the glass, warm lamp amber #f2a65a only as the photograph's own light, white #f5f7fa text, soft grey #a6abb4 secondary text. Status colours only inside interface rows: green #2fa36b "Validé", amber #e9b54c "À valider", red #e0533c "Échec".

Typography: Inter in its display optical size for headlines (medium weight, tight negative tracking, sentence case, never more than two lines), Inter text for body, slightly heavier and slightly tracked on glass for legibility (vibrancy). Buttons: a solid azure capsule for the main action, a clear glass capsule for the secondary one.

Rules: sophistication through restraint, typography and the quality of the light. The glow always comes from a visible source (the lamps, the window, the azure glass), never a floating halo. Absolutely no purple, no violet-to-blue or rainbow gradients, no neon, no glowing orbs, no network or node illustrations, no floating statistic chips, no bento grid, no rows of icon cards, no pill-shaped labels above headlines, no monospace, no emoji, no stock 3D blobs. No customer logos, no testimonials, no star ratings, no usage statistics, no prices. Features not yet available are always marked with the plain word "Bientôt". People and names are fictional examples.
