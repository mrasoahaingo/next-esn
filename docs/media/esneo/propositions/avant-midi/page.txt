Website design concept for "esneo", rendered as a flat, crisp screenshot of a real, finished website (no browser chrome, no device frame, no perspective, no desk scene around it), at the level of an Awwwards Site of the Day. esneo is a French AI SaaS for IT consulting firms (ESN): it extracts consultants' CVs, analyses client mission requests and proposes a weighted positioning score, and a human validates every AI proposal. All visible copy is in French, spelled exactly as quoted, with French accents. The brand appears only as the lowercase wordmark "esneo" in a heavy brush-script style; no other brand, logo or company name anywhere.

Direction "Avant midi": the page tells one Monday morning of an ESN account manager, from a client request arriving at 9 h 02 to the tailored CV ready to send before noon. A huge clock time is the page's monument, set in a heavy condensed grotesk in the spirit of Archivo at its narrowest width (extra bold, tabular figures), always written the French way with a lowercase h, like "9 h 02". The light of the page follows the hour: the opening is a deep blue-graphite dawn, the middle turns to a pale winter sky, the end is bright white daylight, and noon is one solid azure field. Headlines in the same grotesk at normal width, medium weight, tight tracking; body text and interface small and calm.

Palette: graphite #0d0e11, dawn blue #1b2436, winter sky #c9d5e8, light grey #f1f3f6, white #ffffff, and azure #3466e6 for every button and for the noon field. Tiny status colours only inside interface cards: green #17734b "Validé", amber #e9b54c "À valider", red #d0412c "Échec".

Photography: documentary editorial photographs of the same office corner high in a La Défense tower in November, at different hours of the morning (blue dawn with lit windows, first low sun, grey daylight), real people seen naturally, natural skin, no readable text, no logos, no brand names on buildings, no signage. Interface fragments: crisp white product cards at true size (10 px radius, thin light border, small clean sans), readable.

Rules: headlines sentence case, never more than two lines, flush left. Square-cornered buttons and photographs. Absolutely no gradients (except a neutral dark scrim under text on a photo), no glow, no glass, no rows of icon cards, no pill badges or small uppercase labels above headlines, no monospace, no 01/02/03 numbering, no hairline grid lines, no decorative shapes, no stock illustration, no emoji. No customer logos, no testimonials, no star ratings, no usage statistics, no prices. People and names are fictional examples.
