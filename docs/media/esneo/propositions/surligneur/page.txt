Website design concept for "esneo", rendered as a flat, crisp screenshot of a real, finished website (no browser chrome, no device frame, no perspective, no desk scene around it), at the level of an Awwwards Site of the Day. esneo is a French AI SaaS for IT consulting firms (ESN): it extracts consultants' CVs, analyses client mission requests and proposes a weighted positioning score, and a human validates every AI proposal. All visible copy is in French, spelled exactly as quoted, with French accents. The brand appears only as the lowercase wordmark "esneo" in a heavy brush-script style; no other brand, logo or company name anywhere.

Direction "Le surligneur": the page is built from the two documents every ESN account manager reads side by side with a highlighter in hand, the client's mission request and the consultant's CV, set as flat white printed pages. The AI's reading is drawn on them in azure highlighter: flat pale-azure blocks behind key phrases, one active phrase in solid azure with white text, and short notes set in the margin next to each highlight. The human documents and all headlines are set in an elegant news serif in the spirit of Newsreader (medium weight, optical sizing, very tight tracking when large); the AI's margin notes, buttons, navigation and interface are set in a small clean geometric sans in the spirit of Geist, so the two voices are visibly different.

Strict palette, nothing else: white #ffffff pages, light grey #f1f3f6 ground, near-black graphite #0d0e11 ink, azure #3466e6 for highlights and for every button, pale azure tint #d6e0fa for highlighter blocks. Tiny status colours only inside margin notes and interface rows: green #17734b "Validé", amber #e9b54c "À valider", red #d0412c "Échec".

Rules: headlines in the serif, sentence case, never more than two lines, flush left; body text calm, left aligned, generous margins. Square corners on pages and buttons; separate surfaces by a step in tone, not by lines or shadows. Absolutely no gradients, no glow, no glass, no drop shadows, no rows of icon cards, no pill badges or small uppercase labels above headlines, no monospace, no 01/02/03 numbering, no hairline grid lines, no decorative shapes, no stock illustration, no emoji. No customer logos, no testimonials, no star ratings, no usage statistics, no prices. People and names are fictional examples.
