Website design concept for "esneo", rendered as a flat, crisp screenshot of a real, finished website (no browser chrome, no device frame, no perspective, no desk scene around it), at the level of an Awwwards Site of the Day. esneo is a French AI SaaS for IT consulting firms (ESN): it extracts consultants' CVs, analyses client mission requests and proposes a weighted positioning score, and a human validates every AI proposal. All visible copy is in French, spelled exactly as quoted, with French accents. The brand appears only as the lowercase wordmark "esneo" in a heavy brush-script style; no other brand, logo or company name anywhere.

Direction "Le vivier": an ESN's real asset is its pool of consultants, "le vivier". The page is laid out like a contact sheet of that pool: dozens of small identical flat white tiles in strict rows with narrow 8 px gaps, edge to edge, each tile holding only a short name, a role and one skill in small text, all at one small size. Nothing is featured until a client request is chosen; then the three best matches turn solid azure with their score and move to the front of the sheet while the other tiles fade. Huge headlines in a clean geometric sans in the spirit of Geist (medium weight, very tight tracking); everything else in the same sans at one small size, so the page speaks the product's own typeface.

Palette: cool slate-grey ground #dde2e8 (the grey of office partitions and staffing boards), white #ffffff tiles, graphite #0d0e11 ink, a deep slate field #273041 and a graphite field for the close, and azure #3466e6 for the selected tiles and every button. Tiny status colours only as small dots inside tiles: green #17734b "Validé", amber #e9b54c "À valider", red #d0412c "Échec".

Rules: headlines sentence case, never more than two lines, flush left. Square corners on tiles and buttons; tiles are filled, never outlined; surfaces separated by a step in tone, never by lines or shadows. Absolutely no gradients, no glow, no glass, no drop shadows, no icons on tiles, no pill badges or small uppercase labels above headlines, no monospace, no 01/02/03 numbering, no hairline grid lines, no decorative shapes, no stock illustration, no emoji, no portraits on the tiles. No customer logos, no testimonials, no star ratings, no usage statistics, no prices. People and names are fictional examples.
